# LeagueMatch v.0.2

**A competitive tennis matchmaking platform rekindling the thrill of 1v1 rivalry – powered by social, seasonal, and skill-based gameplay.**  
*Built with Nuxt & Supabase (Web2 now, Web3 ready later).*

---

## 🔭 Core Long-Term Vision

### 1. **1v1 League System**
- Local to national ladder rankings  
- Seasonal leagues with playoffs and championship brackets  
- Skill divisions: *Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced / Challenger*  

### 2. **SPIN Matchmaking (Skill-based Player Integration Network)**
- Dynamic rating system (ELO/UTR hybrid)  
- Matchmaking by:
  - Skill proximity  
  - Geographic range  
  - Play style profiling (aggressive baseliner, serve-volley, etc.)  
- Optional: court booking integrations  

### 3. **Player Identity Layer**
- Verified profiles with:
  - Match stats, heatmaps, W/L ratios  
  - Short-form video highlights (self-upload or auto-generated)  
  - Equipment tags (racquet, grip, string tension)  
  - Rivalries, rematch challenges, and favorites  

---

## 💰 Monetization Strategy

### 1. **Freemium Tiers**
- Free: Casual match invites, limited stats  
- Premium ($10/mo or $29/season):  
  - League access  
  - Advanced analytics  
  - Custom badges/profile themes  

### 2. **Sponsorships**
- Tennis brands, gear startups, or local courts sponsor players or tournaments  

### 3. **Event Revenue**
- Take a % from hosted tournaments or court reservations  
- Option to offer paid highlight reels or player promos  

---

## 🧬 Key Differentiators

### 1. **Sport-Centric Design**
- Built for tennis only (for now):  
  - Tracks serve %, rally length, breakpoint conversions  
  - Supports pro sets, match tiebreaks, and custom scoring  

### 2. **Community-Centric Ecosystem**
- Host or join community meetups  
- Book verified coaches  
- Find doubles partners via synergy scores  

### 3. **Path to the Pros**
- Players can build a public portfolio to attract:
  - College recruiters  
  - Local clubs  
  - UTR/ITF wildcards (eventual integration)  

---
